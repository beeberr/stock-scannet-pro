# Project brief — AGOR Creative Engineering

## Deliverable
A luxury brochure-style presentation/PDF explaining the 17 stages of installing a movable pool floor.

## Design requirements
- Exact original order: all 17 stages, with no omissions or merging.
- At least half a page per stage.
- Large, legible typography.
- A large photograph for every stage, used as a background or placed beside the text.
- Premium visual language: deep blue and gold, rich design rather than plain white pages.
- Striking cover and consistent AGOR Creative Engineering branding.
- Preserve the source PDF's professional Hebrew wording, technical instructions, and stated durations.

## Duration facts from the source
- Stage 4: approximately 6 weeks.
- Stage 6: approximately 3–4 months.
- Stage 7: approximately one month, in parallel with manufacturing.
- Stage 11: approximately one week.
- Stage 15: approximately 2–4 days.
- Stage 16: usually approximately two days.
- Do not add these durations into one simple total because some work runs in parallel.
