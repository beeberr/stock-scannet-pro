# Canonical stage register — AGOR movable pool floor

The following is a structured record of the original Hebrew stage descriptions preserved from the source document. The final brochure must retain the full source wording and all technical details; this register is a working reference, not permission to shorten the source PDF.

1. **פגישה ראשונה לפני יציקת הבריכה** — At the end of formwork for pool walls, invite AGOR to participate in pouring one wall adjacent to the machine room, including hydraulic pipes and grounding rod. Follow AGOR instructions for machine room and preparations for air, electrical, control, water, pool basin and surroundings. Verify actions before pouring.
2. **ליווי הפרויקט ברקע** — AGOR remains involved through shell and finish stages, coordinating with supervision and architect. Initial measurement after pool plaster to verify match between order and actual pool.
3. **סיום ריצוף וחיפוי – מדידה סופית** — After interior pool tiling and external flooring around pool (which moving floor must align with at upper position), final measurement can be done. **User instruction for the brochure: explicitly state that the production time for the hydraulic floor is approximately six months.** This is a user-provided schedule clarification for the stage 3 section; reconcile the six-month overall production lead time with the source document's separate stage durations (including stage 6's 3–4 months) before final publication, rather than silently presenting them as competing standalone durations.
4. **מדידת הבריכה והכנת תיק ייצור** — AGOR measures pool and prepares production file. Approx. 6 weeks.
5. **אישור תכנית סופית** — Final plan sent for approval before production. Customer or representative/professionals must sign.
6. **ייצור** — Production, approx. 3–4 months.
7. **הכנות בחדר המכונות** — Complete all preparations according to AGOR written and oral instructions during measurement. While moving floor is being manufactured, complete construction of machine room and install required system equipment there. Approx. one month, run in parallel with manufacture and managed by customer.
8. **הכנת מקום לחיישן הפיקוד (פאנל)** — Prepare control panel location where entire pool and surroundings are visible. Install GEWISS 6 junction box and route control wiring to machine room/area of movable-floor equipment: 10-core cable 10×1.5, per instructions.
9. **ניסוי במפעל** — Factory simulation/test of movable floor operation to verify proper functioning.
10. **הובלה והנפה** — Delivery and crane lift to pool site.
11. **התקנה** — Connect moving floor to pool basin, operating water flow and controls, enabling operation without pool water for leveling and handoff to tiling. Approx. one week.
12. **עבודות הריצוף על במת הרצפה הנעה** — Tiling according to AGOR instructions. Distribute tiles evenly so weight is uniform across moving floor platform; avoid uneven loads that may affect leveling. Distribute all tiles and adhesive evenly in advance, as specified in AGOR tiling appendix, and wait 24 hours before actual tiling.
13. **ביצוע הריצוף על במת הרצפה הנעה** — Perform tiling on moving-floor platform according to AGOR appendix and project manager guidance.
14. **ניקיון הבמה והמערכת** — Remove adhesive and dirt from all work, clean pool basin and moving floor system. Ensure no debris remains, even minor amounts, to prevent corrosion; critical to long-term system integrity.
15. **חיבור הארקה, פילוס במה ופילוס מדרגות** — Grounding connection, platform leveling and step leveling; final actions before filling pool. Approx. 2–4 days.
16. **מילוי מים** — Filling with water; usually approx. two days.
17. **בדיקה סופית ומסירת מערכת הרצפה הנעה** — Final inspection, handover, training and delivery of operating keys. System ready for use.
